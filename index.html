How to Use a Knife Properly

The knife is one of the most important and dangerous utensils in cooking. You will be using a kinfe to peel things, chop things, and in some cases crush things. One of the most important steps to having a safe knife is making sure it is sharp. If you have an unsharpened knife while you are cutting things, you can slip and cut yourself instead! What you want to happen when you are slicing and dicing is a clean cut straight through. One that just glides with the object you are cutting into. Another important thing is the "Claw Method."With the claw method, it ensures that you are using the knife as safe as possible. In order to use the claw method you must first hold the knife with your dominant hand so that you are pinching a part of the knife making it essentially an extension of your arm. With your non-dominant hand you must make your fingers into a claw and place it firmly on the object you are cutting. As you are cutting you want to do a rowing motion with your knife and you must have your "Claw" guide your knife.

Identifying and Using Kitchenware

No one can cook with just there bare hands and some fire. Well. You can't cook EVERYTHING with just fire and your hands. Some recipes, like pasta or anything fried, will need you to use pots, pans, and other stuff like mixing bowls. In order to make a great dish you will need to use all of these items and today I will teach you how to identify each of them as well as when and where to use them. The first two objects that you will need is a cast iron skillet and a non stick frying pan. The cast iron skillet is a very thick pan that can easily be identified from it's almost jet black color and from how heavy it is! The frying pan can be identified by its circular shape with a long straight handle on the end. The frying pan is a classic item in the kitchen and will be used to fry and cook a lot of things like eggs and even some types of meat! The cast iron skillet is very important as it has great heat retention and distribution making it a great choice to sear, fry, and sautee aything your creative mind can think of. Another item is a pot. Pots are very important as it can be the key ingredient to making a wonderful soup or even pasta. Pots are an iconic part of the kitchen and can be identified by their similarity to bowls except they are a little bigger with handles on the sides and a lid to accompany it. When you are making stuff like soup or are boiling stuff like pasta, you want to heat your pot on the stove with water or any other liquid you are using. A lot of the time your pot will be used to boil. In order to know that it is boiling correctly look for bubbles that are forming at the top and that is how you know it is boiling. One of the most important items is a cutting board. The cutting board can be identified by its rectangular shape and the wooden or plastic texture. You will be using this area to chop many things up like vegetables and meat.

Mixing the Textures and Tastes of Food

The most crucial step to making food is making sure that everythin on your plate compliments each other. One example of this is when you take a nice soft bread and you mix it with something crunchy like a nice fluffy french fry. When you bite into that fry at first it is crunchy from the fried skin but then you get a nice soft fluffy pillow from the potatoes on the inside. You need to make sure that the textures compliment each other and make sure not one texture over powers the other. This is the exact same for taste. When making something taste good it is almost all in the seasonings you use. The most iconic seasoning example is salt and pepper. Salt and Pepper are your basis for seasonings in cooking as it is in almost everything. If not both then definitely the other. In order to properly mix seasonings you need to identify what type of flavor it is. There are 5 categories in cooking.

  1. Sweet
  2. Spicy
  3. Earthy
  4. Savory
  5. Bright

Sweetness is things like donuts, vanilla, or strawberrys. These will be used to reward your brain from how good this thing is. Spice is stuff like hot sauce, peppers, and chili powder. You will add spicieness to give your food a certain kick that hurts but is also really good. Earthy flavors is stuff like basil, thime, and oregano. You use these types of seasonings to give it a grounded feel that is more connected to nature. Next up is things that are savory like garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper. You would use these seasonings to give things a rich and balanced foundation. Now that you know all of these things all you have to do is combine them into a way that is comfortable to you.
